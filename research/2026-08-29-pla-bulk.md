# Bulk PLA for LowRider V4

## Requirement

LowRider V4 structural print consumption is roughly **2.7 kg**.

Buy enough for:
- full machine
- failed/reprinted parts
- dust shoe / adapters / fixtures
- useful matching spare material

Practical purchase target: **~6 kg**.

Material rule:
- ordinary stiff PLA is the baseline
- do not choose silk, wood, CF or matte merely because a sale price is attractive
- PLA+ / high-speed is not automatically better for LR4 stiffness

### Why this beats the giant spool as our default

Compared with one 5 kg spool:
- uses standard spool holders/dry boxes
- no special heavy-spool stand/bearings required
- six individually sealed rolls
- easy to keep spare material dry
- Mix & Match allows a deliberate colour scheme
- if one spool has a problem, the entire 5 kg supply is not affected
- ~6 kg gives much more useful margin than exactly 5 kg

The price penalty is small enough that this is the better **snål men inte dumsnål** choice.

## Colour plan

The 6 × 1 kg format lets us buy something like:
- 4–5 kg main structural colour
- 1–2 kg accent colour

without sacrificing bulk economics.

Do not buy six novelty colours just because Mix & Match exists; most LR4 printed mass should ideally stay one predictable ordinary PLA batch/type.

### Why it is now option B, not option A

A 5 kg spool can require:
- a separate low-friction spool holder
- stronger support due to spool mass
- more awkward drying/storage
- one colour for the whole spool

For a user whose priority is getting the LowRider built and used rather than creating a filament-handling side project, saving ~€15 is not enough to make this the default.

Choose it only if:
- single colour is preferred
- printer/feed setup already handles a 5 kg spool comfortably, or
- building a simple external roller holder is desirable anyway

## Rejected tempting option — matte PLA

SUNLU matte bulk is currently extremely cheap on some variants, around €9.99–11.99/kg and sometimes additional bulk discounts.

Do **not** select matte simply to shave a few euros.

Reason: LR4 wants conventional predictable stiff PLA; visual finish is secondary. Matte formulations can modify mechanical behaviour and provide no useful structural advantage here.

Use standard PLA unless a specific mechanical comparison proves otherwise.
