# Research: bulk PLA for LowRider V4

## Goal

Find **reliable ordinary PLA**, not novelty filament, at roughly **100 SEK/kg delivered** if possible.

Structural LR4 parts favour predictable/stiff PLA. Do not optimize around silk, wood-filled, glow, CF, or a matte formulation merely because it is cheap. PLA+ / high-speed variants are acceptable only if there is a real price or availability reason; they are not automatically better for this machine.

Expected LR4 printed-part consumption is roughly 2.7 kg, so practical purchase sizes are 4–6 kg to allow failed prints, calibration and spares.

## Currency reference

Live utility rate checked 2026-08-29:
- 1 EUR = **11.1274 SEK**

SEK/kg approximations below use that rate and are not checkout guarantees.

## 1. SUNLU regular PLA — strongest raw-price candidate

### 5 kg large spool

Fresh Europe-selectable SUNLU listing observed:
- material: regular PLA
- 5 kg large spool
- 1.75 mm ±0.02 mm
- **€39.99** on a fresh Europe-selected variant
- SUNLU states free shipping to most EU regions on its large-spool range; Sweden must still be confirmed at checkout

Math:
- €39.99 / 5 = **€8.00/kg**
- at current reference rate ≈ **89 SEK/kg** before any checkout-specific shipping difference

Source:
- https://store.sunlu.com/sv-fr/products/5kg-large-spool-pla-abs-petg-and-pla-3d-printer-filament

### Important downside: it is one very large spool

This is economically excellent but changes handling:
- one colour only per 5 kg spool
- may require an external low-friction spool holder rather than the printer's normal holder
- a standard dry box may not fit it
- SUNLU explicitly markets the 5 kg format for continuous large jobs and recommends large-format storage / DIY dry-box approaches

SUNLU's published FAQ gives large-spool dimensions that appear physically questionable for 5 kg of PLA, so **do not design a holder from the published dimensions before the spool arrives**. Measure the actual spool or use a tolerant roller-style holder.

Source:
- https://store.sunlu.com/collections/5kg-spool-filament

### Assessment

**Price winner if a single main colour is acceptable and Sweden checkout keeps the advertised EU shipping economics.**

A 5 kg spool is particularly attractive for LR4 because the structural set is a long, high-volume print job and a single batch avoids colour/batch changes.

## Recommended purchase hierarchy

### A — cheapest / strongest current lead

**SUNLU regular PLA 5 kg large spool, Europe variant, ~€39.99**, provided Sweden checkout preserves low/free shipping.

Expected raw price ≈ **89 SEK/kg**.

Best if we decide on a single dominant colour and are willing to use a large-spool holder.

### B — best colour flexibility near target

**Creality Soleyin Ultra 6 kg bulk at €59** when it returns to stock.

~109 SEK/kg, 1 kg-spool convenience, Creality quality. Confirm whether the exact bundle allows chosen colours or is random before ordering.

### C — buy-now normal-spool route

SUNLU regular PLA 1 kg rolls / Mix & Match, targeting the best live 6-roll discount. Likely ~120–135 SEK/kg depending promotion, but easier handling and deliberate colour split.

## Colour implication

Do **not** lock filament format before colour scheme:
- one-colour / industrial monochrome → 5 kg SUNLU large spool is hard to beat
- two-colour machine → compare `5 kg main + 1 kg accent` against `6 × 1 kg selectable-colour` total

A 5 kg main spool plus one ordinary ~€12–13 accent roll would still average roughly **€8.7–8.9/kg over 6 kg** before final shipping, i.e. around the 100 SEK/kg goal, while preserving an accent colour.

## Buy condition

Before ordering:
1. confirm Sweden destination on SUNLU/Creality checkout
2. confirm shipping total
3. confirm exact material is ordinary PLA, not matte/silk/CF
4. confirm stock and colour
5. if using 5 kg spool, accept that an external holder is required / likely required

Current result: **the user's ~100 SEK/kg expectation is realistic; Amazon just is not the best verified route today.**
