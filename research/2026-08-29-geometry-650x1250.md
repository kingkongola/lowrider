# Exact LR4 geometry — 650 × 1250 mm usable area

## Why this size

Target sheet use is quarter of a common 1220×2440 mm sheet:
- quarter sheet = **610×1220 mm**
- 650×1250 usable gives **40 mm extra X** and **30 mm extra Y** beyond that nominal panel size

That is enough margin to avoid designing a machine whose nominal work area is exactly the same as the stock dimensions.

## Purchased XZ plate thickness

HaWiWe explicitly states its aluminium XZ plates are **6 mm thick**.

This matters slightly because V1E's calculator uses XZ plate thickness in X table-width calculation.

Source:
- https://hawiwe.de/produkt/aluminium-xz-platten/

## Current V1E calculator formulas

Current calculator offsets:
- `xrail_core = 169`
- `yrail_minus_work = 255`
- `ytable_minus_work = 313`
- `xbelt_extra = 180`
- `ybelt_extra = 200`
- `xtable_extra = 110`
- `xrail_cushion = 3`

Current formulas:
- `xstrut = ceil(xUsable + 169)`
- `xrails = xstrut - 3` in metric rounding behavior
- `yrail = yUsable + 255`
- `xbelt = xstrut + 180`
- `ybelt = yrail + 200`
- `belt_total = xbelt + 2*ybelt`
- `xtable = xstrut + 2*xzPlateThickness + 110`
- `ytable = yUsable + 313`

## Exact outputs for 650 × 1250 mm, 6.0 mm plates

### Rails / steel tube

- **2 × X rails: 816 mm each**
- **1 × Y rail: 1505 mm**

Total finished tube length:
- 816 + 816 + 1505 = **3137 mm**

Allowing a few mm for two saw cuts, a straight **3.2 m stock length is enough in theory**.

If local supplier economics favour a standard 6 m length, one 6 m tube is more than enough and leaves useful spare material. Do not buy three separate long tubes without checking stock-length pricing.

### Strut plates

- custom strut length: **819 mm**

This value feeds the current V1E/Vector76 strut generator.

### GT2 belt

- X belt: **999 mm**
- Y belt: **1705 mm each ×2**
- exact calculated total: **4409 mm**

Therefore:
- one **5 m** correct 10 mm fiberglass GT2 belt is sufficient
- nominal leftover after exact calculated lengths: **591 mm**

This confirms the LaskaKit 5 m belt plan and supersedes generic ~8 m BOM quantities for this specific machine.

### Minimum table footprint

With the purchased 6.0 mm XZ plates:
- minimum X table width: **941 mm**
- minimum Y table length: **1563 mm**

This is the calculator's outside footprint where belt holders sit flush with the outside corners.

V1E recommends adding roughly 25–50 mm or more to the table dimensions for protection / wall clearance.

A practical target of about **1000 × 1620 mm** therefore remains very sensible:
- +59 mm total in X over minimum
- +57 mm total in Y over minimum
- roughly 28–30 mm practical margin per side/end if centred

Source:
- https://docs.v1e.com/lowrider/calculator/
- https://docs.v1e.com/lowrider/

## Tube specification

V1E accepts:
- OD **29.5 mm, 30 mm or 32 mm**
- OD tolerance ±0.2 mm
- wall thickness at least **1.3 mm**
- steel, stainless or DOM steel

V1E explicitly says very thick walls / solid rods add little useful rigidity and mostly add mass/cost.

For Swedish sourcing the current practical target remains:
- **32 mm OD precision steel tube**
- around **2 mm wall** if price is good

Do not pay a major premium for very thick stainless merely for perceived rigidity.

## Recommendation

**Formally lock 650 × 1250 mm usable cutting area unless a new garage-space constraint appears.**

Reasons:
1. comfortably accepts nominal 610×1220 quarter sheets
2. keeps the machine/table compact at roughly 1000×1620 mm
3. only needs 3137 mm finished rail tube
4. only needs 4409 mm GT2 belt, so a 5 m roll works
5. avoids spending money and garage footprint on unused extra X/Y travel

## Purchase consequences once locked

Immediately unlocks:
- LaskaKit 5 m GT2 belt purchase
- steel tube sourcing at exact lengths 816/816/1505 mm
- strut generator at 819 mm
- table-base search around a ~1000×1620 mm CNC top

Still do not build/cut the final table until the chosen used/new underframe is measured and the dust-hose routing is considered.
