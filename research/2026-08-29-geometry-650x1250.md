# Exact LR4 geometry — 650 × 1250 mm usable area

## Why this size

Target stock use is quarter of a common 1220×2440 mm sheet:
- quarter sheet = **610×1220 mm**
- 650×1250 usable gives **40 mm extra X** and **30 mm extra Y**

## Purchased XZ plate thickness

HaWiWe states its aluminium XZ plates are **6 mm thick**. V1E's calculator uses XZ thickness in X table-width calculation.

## Current V1E calculator formulas

Current offsets:
- `xrail_core = 169`
- `yrail_minus_work = 255`
- `ytable_minus_work = 313`
- `xbelt_extra = 180`
- `ybelt_extra = 200`
- `xtable_extra = 110`
- `xrail_cushion = 3`

Formulas:
- `xstrut = ceil(xUsable + 169)`
- `xrails = xstrut - 3` in metric behavior
- `yrail = yUsable + 255`
- `xbelt = xstrut + 180`
- `ybelt = yrail + 200`
- `belt_total = xbelt + 2*ybelt`
- `xtable = xstrut + 2*xzPlateThickness + 110`
- `ytable = yUsable + 313`

Source:
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/V1EngineeringInc/V1EngineeringInc-Docs/master/docs/lowrider/calculator.js

## Exact outputs for 650 × 1250 mm, 6.0 mm plates

### Rails / steel tube

- **2 × X rails: 816 mm each**
- **1 × Y rail: 1505 mm**
- finished total: **3137 mm**

Two 2 m stock tubes work physically:
- one → 1505 mm + remainder
- one → 816 + 816 mm + kerf + remainder

### Strut plates

- calculator `strut_length`: **819 mm**
- for chosen Ø30 mm rails: generator `front_wing_size = 30`
- V1E notes the generator intentionally makes the resulting plate about 0.5 mm smaller; do not compensate manually

### GT2 belt

- X belt: **999 mm**
- Y belts: **1705 mm ×2**
- exact total: **4409 mm**
- one **5 m** correct 10 mm GT2 belt leaves **591 mm** nominal margin

### Minimum table footprint

With 6.0 mm XZ plates:
- minimum X table width: **941 mm**
- minimum Y table length: **1563 mm**

Practical deck target ~**1000 × 1620 mm** gives roughly 28–30 mm additional margin per side/end when centered.

V1E's table measurement is the outside footprint of the belt holders. Y-rail/belt clips use the outside edge as reference, so a deck overhang must still be structurally supported in this edge zone; see `TABLE.md` and `AUDIT.md`.

## Tube specification and current choice

V1E accepts:
- OD **29.5, 30 or 32 mm**
- OD tolerance ±0.2 mm
- wall thickness at least **1.3 mm**
- steel, stainless or DOM steel

**Current locked sourcing choice:** Motonet Ø**30×1,5 mm** steel tube, not the older provisional 32 mm target.

Physical gate before cutting:
- measure OD with calipers
- check straightness
- reject visibly dented/bent stock

All diameter-dependent printed parts must therefore be the **30 mm LR4 variant**.

## Decision

**650 × 1250 mm usable area remains verified and locked.**

Consequences:
- rör 816 / 816 / 1505 mm
- 5 m 10 mm GT2 belt
- strut generator 819 / 30 mm wing
- practical deck ~1000×1620 mm
- 30 mm printed rail-dependent parts

Do not change these values merely because an older research file or generic LR4 BOM uses a different standard size.
