## Arrival test before cutting

Before trusting the collet in CNC service:
1. unplug router
2. clean spindle taper, collet and nut
3. verify MRP-1250 seats naturally without forcing
4. install a straight 1/8 end mill with sensible insertion depth
5. hand-rotate first and inspect seating
6. run at low speed and check visible runout
7. ideally measure runout with dial indicator near the collet and farther down a known-straight shank
8. never mix a 6 mm cutter into a 1/4 collet or vice versa

If the Elaire collet does not seat correctly, stop; do not tighten harder to make it fit.
