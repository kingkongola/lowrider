## Arrival test

Before CNC use:
1. unplug router
2. clean taper, nut and collet
3. verify MRP-1250 seats naturally
4. install a straight 1/8 in cutter at correct insertion depth
5. hand rotate / visually inspect
6. run low speed
7. ideally measure runout with dial indicator
8. stop rather than forcing the collet if seating is wrong
