## Belt length for current 650 × 1250 mm target

Using V1E's current calculator formula:
- `xstrut = ceil(xUsable + 169)`
- `xbelt = xstrut + 180`
- `yrail = yUsable + 255`
- `ybelt = yrail + 200`
- total belt = `xbelt + 2 * ybelt`

For 650 × 1250 mm usable area:
- X belt = 999 mm
- each Y belt = 1705 mm
- total = **4409 mm**

Therefore a **single 5 m roll is enough**, leaving ~591 mm spare. This is materially better than blindly buying the generic ~8 m quantity often listed in the LR4 BOM.

Source:
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/V1EngineeringInc/V1EngineeringInc-Docs/master/docs/lowrider/calculator.js

## Amazon / Prime result

No indexed Amazon.se listing was found that could be verified end-to-end as the exact LR4 combination:
- 16T
- 5 mm bore
- 10 mm belt
- plus correct smooth 5 mm-bore idlers
- plus fiberglass 10 mm belt

Therefore do **not** buy an Amazon bundle merely because its title says GT2. The most common traps are 6 mm belt variants, toothed 20T idlers instead of smooth idlers, and wrong bore sizes.

If a specific Amazon listing is found manually in the app, verify the selected variant against these invariants before ordering.

## Current conclusion

Do not place a GT2-only order yet.

Best current structure:
1. Treat **5 m** as sufficient belt quantity for the current 650 × 1250 mm machine.
2. Keep LaskaKit as the strongest candidate for **6 smooth idlers + 5 m fiberglass belt**.
3. Do not accept LaskaKit's 6 mm-width 16T pulley.
4. Keep Hellas / Anodas / Allegro as exact 16T candidates.
5. Before buying, continue commodity-cart research because LaskaKit also stocks other relevant mechanical parts. Spreading the €8.93 Sweden shipping over T8/couplers/endstops or other correct parts may make it the best total cart.

The goal remains lowest correct delivered total, not fewest supplier names at any cost.
