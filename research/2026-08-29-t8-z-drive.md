# Research: T8 Z drive — 2026-08-29

Scope intentionally limited to:
- 2 × Tr8×8 / T8 Z leadscrews
- 2 × matching brass nuts
- 2 × flexible 5→8 mm couplers

## LR4 requirements

Verified against current V1E documentation:
- 2 × T8 leadscrew + nut
- 145 mm or longer
- 4-start
- 2 mm thread pitch
- 8 mm lead / revolution
- 2 × 5 mm → 8 mm couplers

V1E's own spare set is specified as 2 × 145 mm T8 4-start, 2 mm pitch, 8 mm/rev + nuts.

Sources:
- https://docs.v1e.com/lowrider/
- https://www.v1e.com/products/lowrider-v4-cnc-t8-leadscrews

## Best consolidation find so far: one 400 mm Tr8×8 rod from LaskaKit

LaskaKit `LA190032A`:
- T8x8 trapezoidal rod
- 400 mm long
- 4 threaded starts / T8x8 geometry
- stainless steel 304
- ~€8.19 incl VAT
- stock observed 2026-08-29

A single 400 mm rod can be cut into two approximately 199 mm pieces after saw kerf. That is comfortably above V1E's 145 mm minimum, so there is no need to buy two separate 200 mm rods.

Cutting note:
- cut once near the centre
- deburr and lightly chamfer both freshly cut ends before threading into the brass nuts / couplers
- stainless is tougher than mild steel but this is a trivial hacksaw/angle-grinder cut

Source:
- https://www.laskakit.cz/en/trapezova-tyc-t8x8-400mm/

## Matching nuts from the same cart

LaskaKit `LA190033A`:
- brass T8x8 nut
- 4-start / T8x8 geometry
- flange OD 22 mm
- body OD 10 mm
- 15 mm height
- 11×11 mm mounting-hole spacing
- 3.5 mm mounting holes
- ~€1.32 each

Need 2 = ~€2.64.

This matches the standard flanged brass-nut form used by LR4. V1E's build documentation explicitly installs a brass T8 nut into the printed Z_Nut part using 1–2 M3 screws, brass face down.

Source:
- https://www.laskakit.cz/en/matice-pro-trapezovou-tyc-t8x8--mosaz/

Important trap: LaskaKit also sells a visually similar `T8x2` single-start nut. That is wrong for LR4. Buy `LA190033A` T8x8, not T8x2.
